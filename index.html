About Astraea

Procurement informed by public-sector IT experience.

Astraea Industries LLC is a New Jersey-based small business focused on commercial IT hardware procurement for government and education customers.

Our background includes hands-on experience in public-sector IT environments, endpoint deployment, device lifecycle management, networking, and enterprise technology support. That perspective helps us understand the operational requirement behind the equipment—not simply the line item.

Astraea is built around a straightforward standard: accurate sourcing, responsive communication, competitive procurement, and dependable execution.
