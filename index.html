About Astraea

Procurement informed by public-sector IT experience.

Astraea Industries LLC is a New Jersey-based small business focused on commercial IT procurement and lifecycle planning for public-sector organizations.

Our technical background includes hands-on experience in public-sector IT environments, endpoint deployment, device lifecycle management, networking, and enterprise technology support. That perspective helps us understand the operational context behind a technology requirement—not just the line item.

We are building Astraea around a simple standard: accurate sourcing, transparent communication, and dependable execution.
